## Naming Requirements

- Globally pronounceable across English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese
- Easy to spell from hearing it spoken
- Memorable and distinctive
- Suitable for an international technology company
- Not restricted to university applications — must support expansion into credentials, scholarships, institutional software, and education infrastructure
- Preferably available as a `.com` domain
- No conflicts with existing education technology companies

## Top 5 Recommendations

| Rank | Name | Category | Key Strength | Risk Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Admio** | Abstract neologism | Immediately signals admissions; modern; short; globally pronounceable | Low |
| **2** | **Talera** | Abstract neologism | Warm, human; works across languages; not sector-limiting; unique | Low |
| **3** | **Compassia** | Metaphorical | Perfect navigation metaphor; globally pronounceable; distinctive | Low-Medium |
| **4** | **Oresa** | Modern tech | Highly distinctive; premium brand feel; unlikely conflicts | Low |
| **5** | **Navio** | Modern tech | Beautiful navigation word; perfect in Romance languages; memorable | Medium |

### Why These Five

1. **Admio** — The safest bet. It's short, clearly connected to admissions, modern, and highly likely to be clear of education trademark conflicts. It's the name you can explain in one second.

2. **Talera** — The aspirational choice. It evokes talent and a new era without being limiting. It works beautifully across languages and leaves room for the platform to grow beyond admissions into credentials, careers, and education infrastructure.

3. **Compassia** — The mission-driven choice. The compass metaphor perfectly captures "navigating the complexity of global higher education." It's warm, global, and memorable.

4. **Oresa** — The blank-canvas choice. If you want to build a brand from scratch with no prior associations (like Google, Stripe, or Notion), this gives you maximum flexibility.

5. **Navio** — The multilingual choice. It's a real word (ship/navigate) in Portuguese and Spanish — meaningful to Romance language speakers immediately — while being novel and distinctive in English.

---

## Naming Strategy Recommendation

**Primary direction:** Abstract neologisms (Admio, Talera, Oresa). These have the highest probability of trademark clearance, domain availability, and social handle availability.

**Secondary direction:** Metaphorical names (Compassia, Navio). Higher brand resonance but slightly higher risk of existing uses.

**Avoid:** Real English words, common Latin roots already used in tech (Meridian, Lumina, Praxis), and any name containing "Uni-" (overused in education branding).
